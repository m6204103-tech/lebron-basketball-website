About LeBron James

LeBron James is widely considered one of the greatest players in basketball history due to an unprecedented combination of peak dominance, versatile skill, and unmatched longevity. His historic basketball journey began when he was drafted first overall in 2003 by his hometown Cleveland Cavaliers, quickly earning Rookie of the Year honors. Seeking his first championship, he famously relocated to the Miami Heat from 2010 to 2014, capturing back-to-back NBA titles in 2012 and 2013 alongside Dwyane Wade and Chris Bosh. In 2014, he returned to Cleveland and fulfilled his promise to the city by orchestrating a historic 2016 comeback from a 3-1 Finals deficit against a 73-win Warriors team. He entered a new chapter in 2018 with the Los Angeles Lakers, leading them to a 2020 championship, breaking the NBA's all-time scoring record, and making history by playing alongside his son, Bronny James. Now playing in his historic 24th NBA season after signing a two-year deal with the Philadelphia 76ers in July 2026, "King James" continues to extend a legendary resume that boasts four NBA championships, four regular-season MVPs, four Finals MVPs, and three Olympic gold medals. Operating as the ultimate "point forward" with over 43,000 career points, his ability to dominate all facets of the game for over two decades anchors his case as a foundational pillar of the sport.
